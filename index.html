Hello, I’m Albin. This digital visual journal is a sanctuary for the moments, quiet roads, passing light, and fleeting human reflections I’ve captured.

I believe photography isn't about staged perfection; it's about honoring the ordinary — the solitary tree at dusk, the monsoon mist over Kerala hills, and the subtle whispers of everyday life.
